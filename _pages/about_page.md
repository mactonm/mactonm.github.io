I am a Ph.D. student at [Brown University](https://www.brown.edu/) working at the intersection of **Machine Learning,** **Natural Language Processing,** and **AI Evaluation**.

My research asks: **How should AI systems be evaluated when benchmark performance is insufficient evidence for real-world deployment?**

I develop methods for evaluating AI systems beyond static benchmarks, studying how evidence should accumulate from model validation through real-world deployment. My work focuses particularly on **healthcare**, **multilingual AI**, and **underrepresented settings**, where datasets are limited, deployment contexts differ substantially from model-development environments, and conventional large-scale evaluations may be difficult or infeasible.

A central goal of my research is to develop **practical evaluation frameworks that identify when an AI system is ready for the next stage of deployment—and when it is not.**
